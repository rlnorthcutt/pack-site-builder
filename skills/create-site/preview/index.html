Why SiteTheme

Classless baseline

Ivy styles semantic HTML automatically — no utility soup for base typography, forms, tables, and code.

12-column layout

Lattice gives you responsive grids and spacing utilities with clean, predictable breakpoints.

Flash-free dark mode

The <dark-mode-toggle> component persists your choice and syncs across tabs, with a prepaint script to avoid any flash on load.
